Who We Are

Headquartered in Dubai. Growing Across the Region.

Headquartered in Dubai with a growing regional presence across the UAE, Oman, Saudi Arabia, and India, Trident MEA is a leading technology consulting, systems integration, healthcare technology, and managed services provider. We combine strategic consulting, engineering excellence, and trusted global technology partnerships to deliver secure, scalable, and future-ready solutions tailored to each customer's business objectives.

Our expertise spans Enterprise Digital Infrastructure, Cybersecurity, Customer Experience Solutions, Healthcare & Biomedical Technologies, Professional & Managed Services, and AI, Automation & Digital Innovation. By bringing these capabilities together, we create integrated technology environments that help organizations modernize operations, accelerate digital transformation, and achieve measurable business outcomes.
